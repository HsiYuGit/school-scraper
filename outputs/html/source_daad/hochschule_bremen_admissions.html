The ISSC curriculum devotes six semesters to modules covering maritime business related to subjects including maritime economics, management, accounting, ship finance and chartering, Furthermore, the curriculum ensures that ISSC graduates possess broad knowledge of laws relevant to the maritime industry: shipping law and environmental regulation, commercial law, carriage of goods by sea and transport law, charter parties, marine insurance, and maritime arbitration.
